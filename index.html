Projects

IoT botnet detection with a VAE and XGBoost

TensorFlow, XGBoost, scikit-learn

A variational autoencoder compresses 115 network features into 20 latent dimensions. XGBoost then classifies each flow into 11 classes: benign traffic and five Mirai and five Gafgyt attack types.

87.83% multi-class accuracy on 1.41 million held-out flows from the N-BaIoT dataset.

Hospital management system with disease prediction

Python, NumPy, Flask, PHP, MySQL

A web platform for appointments, medical records and role-based dashboards for patients, doctors and administrators. A multilayer perceptron written from scratch in NumPy predicts one of 41 diseases from the symptoms a user selects, and is served through a Flask API.

Real-time tennis ball tracking with OpenCV

Python, OpenCV, Tkinter

Compares the CSRT and KCF trackers with automatic detection by HSV color segmentation. The original video and both tracking results run side by side in a desktop application.

CSRT is the most accurate at about 25 fps. KCF runs at about 60 fps.
